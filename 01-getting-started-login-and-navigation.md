# Getting Started, Login, and Navigation

## Before you begin

You need:

- the CRM web address supplied by your administrator;
- an active user account;
- your registered work email address;
- your password; and
- the correct role, primary branch, and visibility scope for your work.

Use a current desktop browser. The application adapts for smaller screens and can be used during field visits, but data-heavy tables and administration are easiest on a desktop or laptop.

## Signing in

### What it is

Signing in confirms your identity and opens only the records and actions you are allowed to use.

### How to sign in

1. Open the CRM address.
2. Enter your **Email address**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
   - Password characters are hidden by default.
   - Select the eye icon inside the field to temporarily show the password, and select it again to hide the password.
4. Select **Sign in**.
5. The application opens the Dashboard after a successful sign-in.

Email addresses are not case-sensitive, but the password is. Do not share passwords or allow another person to work under your account; important actions are recorded against the signed-in user.
Before signing in, hide the password again if another person can see your screen.

### If sign-in fails

Check that:

- the email has no typing error or extra space;
- Caps Lock is not changing the password;
- you are using the correct CRM environment; and
- your account has not been deactivated.

Repeatedly trying an incorrect password may temporarily slow or block further attempts. If the problem remains, contact the administrator. Self-service password recovery is intentionally not available from the sign-in screen.

## Password help

The **Forgot password** link is intentionally not shown in this CRM. If you cannot sign in, contact an administrator or your reporting manager. They can follow the organization’s approved access-recovery process without exposing a self-service reset option to every visitor.

## Account activation and deactivation

An administrator creates and activates user accounts. When an account is deactivated:

- the user cannot sign in;
- any existing signed-in session is ended immediately; and
- the user's historical activity remains in the CRM for audit purposes.

Deactivation is normally used when someone leaves, changes responsibilities, or temporarily should not access the CRM. It does not delete the person’s past work.

## Understanding the application layout

After signing in, the application has three main areas:

1. **Navigation menu** — opens the modules available to your role.
2. **Top bar** — shows your current role, a bell icon, and sign-out control.
3. **Main content area** — shows the current page, filters, forms, tables, and actions.

The navigation may contain:

- Dashboard
- Lead inbox
- Accounts
- Pipeline
- Tasks
- Approvals
- Contracts
- Reports
- Administration

The menu is permission-aware. For example, a commercial approver may see Approvals while a salesperson may not. A missing menu item normally means that your account does not have permission for it.

## Top-bar controls

### Role label

The role label identifies your primary displayed role. A user can have more than one role, and the permissions from those roles work together. The label may therefore show only one of the roles that contributes to your access.

### Bell icon and notifications

The bell shows the number of unread notifications. Select it to open the latest notifications, including lead assignments, task assignments, due-task reminders, and renewal reminders. Select an item to mark it read and open its related page when a link is available. Use Tasks as the detailed work list after opening a task notification.

### Manage signed-in sessions

Select the sessions icon in the profile area to see where your account is signed in. You can revoke another session if, for example, you signed in on a computer you no longer control. Revoking the current session signs you out immediately. Use this promptly if you suspect someone else has access to your account.

### Sign out

Use the sign-out control when finishing your work, especially on a shared computer. Closing the browser is not a substitute for signing out on a shared device.

## Opening and returning from records

Names and rows in lists are usually links to a detail page. For example:

- select a company in Lead inbox to open the lead;
- select an organization in Accounts to open the account;
- select an opportunity card or row to open the opportunity;
- select a quote to open its commercial details; and
- select a contract to open its status and renewal information.

Use the application’s back link when one is shown, or your browser Back button. Avoid opening the same form in several tabs and saving from each tab, because the later save may be based on older information.

## Common page controls

### Search

Enter the requested text and press **Enter** when the field instructs you to do so. Search behavior differs by module:

- Leads searches lead/company and contact information.
- Accounts searches legal name, trade name, and GSTIN.
- Pipeline searches opportunity information.

Clear the text and press Enter or select **Refresh** to return to the unfiltered list.

### Filters

Filters narrow the list without changing any record. Lead inbox includes status, source, requested service, branch, owner (when permitted), received-date range, response-overdue, **Not yet contacted**, and **Possible duplicates** filters. Tasks includes Open, Completed, Cancelled, and All status views. Open tasks can also be filtered by All open, Today, Overdue, and Upcoming.

### Refresh

Refresh reloads the latest information from the CRM. Use it after another user has changed a shared record or when a background process is expected to update a status.

### Pagination

Lead inbox shows 25 records at a time and provides page controls. Most other current list pages load up to 100 records and do not show page controls. If a needed record is not visible, use search where available or ask an administrator to confirm the record is within your visibility scope.

### Sorting

The current tables do not provide clickable column sorting. Lists use their built-in ordering, such as contract expiry order or pipeline stage order.

### Create, edit, and save

Required fields are identified in the form. Save buttons remain the final confirmation that you want to create or update the record. If validation fails, correct the field highlighted by the form and try again.

### Archive and delete

The CRM does not permanently delete core business records from normal screens. Authorized users can instead select **Archive** on a lead, account, contact, location, or eligible opportunity. Archiving:

- asks for a business reason;
- removes the record from normal active lists and detail access;
- preserves its database and audit history; and
- may be blocked when the record is still used by commercial or operational records.

There is currently no archived-record list or Restore button, so review the confirmation message carefully. Archive is not a substitute for the correct business status. For example, mark a genuine unsuccessful opportunity **Lost** instead of archiving it merely because it was not won.

Other safer business actions include:

- deactivating a user;
- cancelling a task;
- disabling an approval policy;
- marking a deal lost; or
- keeping old quote and contract versions in history.

This preserves reporting and audit history.

## Permissions and record visibility

Two checks affect access:

- **Permission** answers “May this user perform this type of action?”
- **Scope** answers “Which records may this user see or change?”

A salesperson may have permission to view leads but only see leads they own. A branch manager may see records for assigned branches. A regional manager may see records for branches in assigned regions. Organization-wide roles may see all relevant records.

If a link from another person returns a not-found or access message, the record may be outside your scope. Do not ask someone to copy restricted information. Ask your manager or administrator to review ownership and scope.

## A practical first-day checklist

1. Sign in and confirm that the expected menu items appear.
2. Open the Dashboard and review your assigned and overdue work.
3. Open Tasks and check Today and Overdue.
4. Open Lead inbox and confirm that your branch records are visible.
5. Open one permitted lead, account, and opportunity to learn the detail layout.
6. Confirm with your manager which stages, response targets, and approval process your team follows.
7. Sign out before leaving a shared computer.

## Important notes

- Never put passwords, card information, or unrelated sensitive personal data into notes.
- Use verified dates and amounts because they affect dashboards, reports, approvals, and reminders.
- If your role or branch changes, an administrator must update your CRM account.
- Browser access does not override CRM permissions. Direct links are checked in the same way as navigation menus.
