# Dashboard

## What the Dashboard is

The Dashboard is the daily starting page. It summarizes new work, deadlines, and the visible sales pipeline. It does not show organization-wide information unless your role and scope allow organization-wide access.

## Why and when to use it

Use the Dashboard at the beginning of the day and after important updates. It helps answer:

- Which new leads need attention?
- Which response deadlines have already passed?
- What work is due today or overdue?
- How many opportunities are in each pipeline stage?
- What expected value is currently in the pipeline?

The Dashboard is a work-prioritization tool. Use the underlying Lead inbox, Tasks, and Pipeline pages for full record details.

## Dashboard statistics

### New leads

**Meaning:** Leads currently in the **New** status within your visibility scope.

**Why it matters:** New enquiries should be reviewed and contacted promptly.

**How to use it:** Select the statistic to open Lead inbox filtered to new leads. Open each lead, verify its information, and log the first contact.

**Example:** If the card shows 4, four visible leads have not yet progressed beyond New.

### Response overdue

**Meaning:** Visible leads whose response target has passed without the required first response.

**Why it matters:** A late first response can reduce the chance of winning the work and may breach the agreed service level.

**How to use it:** Select the statistic to open Lead inbox with the overdue filter. Contact the customer, then record a Call, Email, or Meeting activity so the first-contact time is captured.

**Important note:** Recording an internal note does not count as contacting the customer.

### Due today

**Meaning:** Open tasks whose due date/time falls today.

**How to use it:** Select the statistic to open Tasks filtered to Today. Complete the customer action first, then mark the task complete with a meaningful outcome.

### Tasks overdue

**Meaning:** Open tasks whose due date/time has already passed.

**How to use it:** Select the statistic to open Tasks filtered to Overdue. Complete, reschedule, or cancel each item as appropriate. Do not complete a task simply to remove it from the list if the work was not actually done.

## Pipeline by stage

This section groups your visible opportunities by the configured pipeline stage. Each stage shows:

- the number of opportunities; and
- the combined expected amount.

Select **Open pipeline** to work with opportunity cards or the pipeline table.

### Example

Suppose the Dashboard shows:

- Proposal Sent — 3 opportunities — ₹12,00,000
- Negotiation — 2 opportunities — ₹7,50,000

This means five visible opportunities currently sit in those two stages. It does not mean that ₹19,50,000 has been earned or contracted. The values are sales expectations and may change.

## Keep momentum panel

The **Keep momentum** area directs attention to follow-ups. Select **Review my follow-ups** to open Tasks. This is useful when dashboard counts look healthy but individual next actions still need to be completed.

## Recommended daily workflow

1. Open **Response overdue** and handle late first responses.
2. Open **Tasks overdue** and resolve missed follow-ups.
3. Open **Due today** and plan the remaining day.
4. Review **New leads** and confirm ownership and service interest.
5. Review **Pipeline by stage** for stalled or high-value deals.
6. Refresh the Dashboard after recording important changes.

## What affects the numbers

Dashboard values change when users:

- create or receive leads;
- record the first customer contact;
- convert leads;
- complete, cancel, or reschedule tasks;
- edit opportunity amounts or probability;
- change opportunity stages; or
- gain or lose visibility to branches, regions, or records.

Because these values are live operational summaries, two users can legitimately see different totals.

## Troubleshooting

**A card shows zero even though someone else sees records.**  
Your ownership, branch, region, or role scope may differ from theirs.

**A lead remains overdue after I added a note.**  
Record a customer-contact activity such as Call, Email, or Meeting. An internal note is not a first response.

**A completed task is no longer shown in Open.**  
Open Tasks and select **Completed** under Task status. The completed task shows its completion time and recorded outcome. Select **Cancelled** for cancelled work or **All** for all statuses.

**The numbers did not change immediately.**  
Select Refresh or reload the page. If the value remains wrong, verify the underlying record before reporting a problem.
